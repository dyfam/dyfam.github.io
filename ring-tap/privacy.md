# Privacy Policy — RING TAP

**Effective date:** July 12, 2026
**Last updated:** September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RING TAP ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses,
and shares information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information:

| Data | Purpose | Collected via |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous account ID | Identify your device across sessions so your best score and friends list persist | Supabase Anonymous Authentication (generated on first launch, not tied to any personal account) |
| Nickname | Display your name on the leaderboard | You choose it in-app; you can change it anytime |
| Score | Power the global and friends leaderboards | Recorded automatically when you finish a game |
| Friend code | Let you add friends by sharing a 6-character code | Generated automatically per anonymous account |
| Advertising identifier | Serve and measure ads | Google AdMob (see Section 3) |
| Google account email address (**optional**) | Back up your account so you can restore your nickname, scores and friends after reinstalling | Only if you choose to link a Google account in the leaderboard screen (see Section 3) |

Everything except the last row is collected for every player. The Google account
email address is collected **only if you actively choose** to back up your account
with Google; linking is entirely optional and every feature of the App works
without it.

We do **not** collect your name, phone number, precise location, or contact list.
The App does not require you to create an account with a password, and we never
collect your Google password — the sign-in is handled by Google.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To operate the global and friends leaderboards (FR-LB).
- To let you compete with friends via friend codes.
- To detect when a friend has passed your best score (shown as an in-app badge on
  your next launch).
- To display ads that support the free app, and to measure ad performance.
- If you choose to link a Google account, to identify your backup so that your
  nickname, scores and friends can be restored on a new device or after
  reinstalling. We do not use your email address to contact you, and we do not
  send marketing messages.

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App shows ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video) through
[Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543). AdMob may collect
and use advertising identifiers and other data as described in
[Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings
(e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, "Limit Ad Tracking" /
App Tracking Transparency on iOS).

### Google Sign-In (Optional account backup)

If you tap "Back up with Google" in the leaderboard screen, the App uses Google
Sign-In to link your anonymous account to your Google account. We receive only the
email address of the Google account you select; we do not receive your password,
and we do not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google
service. See [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

This step is optional. If you never tap it, no Google account information is
collected and nothing else about the App changes.

### Supabase (Backend)

Leaderboard data (anonymous ID, nickname, score, friend relationships) is stored
with [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), our backend infrastructure
provider. If you link a Google account, the linked email address is also stored
with Supabase as part of your authentication record. Supabase does not receive any
data beyond what is listed in Section 1.

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your data is retained as long as the App is installed and your anonymous
  account remains active.
- Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your server-side data
  (the anonymous account is tied to the device's auth session, not the install).
- If you linked a Google account, uninstalling does not delete your data either;
  that is the point of the backup — signing in with the same Google account
  restores your nickname, scores and friends.
- You may delete your account and associated data at any time, either in the app
  or by emailing us. See Section 5 for both routes and for exactly what is removed.

## 5. How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your RING TAP account and everything stored with it at any time,
through either of the following routes.

### In the app

Open the leaderboard screen and tap **"Delete my data"** at the bottom. Confirm the
prompt. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

### By request (if you have already uninstalled the app)

Email **dyfam.contact@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Delete my RING TAP
account"**. To let us find your account, include your **6-character friend code**
if you still have it, or the **Google account email address** you linked if you
used the optional backup. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days
of receiving your request.

### What gets deleted

- Your account and its anonymous identifier
- Your nickname and friend code
- Your scores and leaderboard entries
- Your friend list
- The linked Google account email address, if you used the optional backup

### What does not get deleted

- The best score saved locally on your own device. It is not part of your account
  and never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app removes it.
- Aggregate, non-identifying statistics that cannot be traced back to you.

### When it is deleted

Your account and the data listed above are removed from our active systems
**immediately** when you delete in the app, or **within 30 days** of us receiving
an emailed request. Copies held in our provider's encrypted backups are purged on
their normal rotation and in no case are retained longer than **30 days** after
deletion. We keep no identifying data about you after that point.

Deleting your account does not require you to stop using RING TAP. The next time
you open the app, a fresh anonymous account is created and you can play normally.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13.
